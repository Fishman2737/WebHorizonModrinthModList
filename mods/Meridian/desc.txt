What it is:
Meridian, a shader pack in Horizon's own format (WGSL, shader API 0.1).
• Shadow-mapped sun: trees, mobs and the player (first person too) cast soft shadows, whichever way you look
• Clear water: see the bottom, waves, refraction, caustics, shore foam, reflections of the world
• Coloured block light (soul fire blue, redstone red, amethyst violet), light through leaves, wet ground in the rain, ambient occlusion, LabPBR maps
• Scattered sky, high clouds, stars, Milky Way, aurora over cold biomes; underwater / Nether / End atmospheres
• Bloom, depth of field, motion blur, eye adaptation, filmic tone map, TAA
• One-click Quality preset (Low → Ultra) + 58 options on Horizon's Shader Options screen

Install: drop the .hshader into your shaderpacks folder → Options → Horizon Settings → Shader Packs → Meridian.

Horizon version: Nightly 2026-11-01 (c9d474d) or later. A compat pack for 0.2.97 (fewer effects) is in the same release.
