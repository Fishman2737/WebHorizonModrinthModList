A food and farming mod for the Horizon Minecraft client, 
in the spirit of Farmer's Delight: a cooking pot, a cutting board, crops you grow and find wild, feasts, pies, placeable dishes and plenty of storage.
DEPENDENCIES: 
https://github.com/realblobii/cookpot
https://github.com/realblobii/roast
  
  ( MUST BUILD YOURSELF )
