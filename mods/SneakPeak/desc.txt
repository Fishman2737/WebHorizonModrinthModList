Hover a shulker box to see what's inside: its 27 slots appear as a 9 x 3 grid right in the tooltip, laid out exactly like the box's own screen, with item icons and stack counts.

Works with the plain shulker box and all 16 dyed ones, whether empty, partly filled or full.
Works on every screen with item tooltips: your inventory, chests and other containers, the creative inventory, the item panel, chat item links and trading.
Keeps the tooltip's usual lines (name, lore, "64 x Diamond"...); the grid goes underneath.
Respects hidden tooltips: a box whose minecraft:tooltip_display hides its contents stays hidden.
Settings in Options > Mods > ShulkerPeek > Settings: show the grid always or only while holding Shift, show a grid for empty boxes, and tint the grid with the box's dye colour.
Uses your resource pack's own shulker box texture.
Works in single player and on LAN worlds you host. Horizon currently leaves a box's contents out of what its built-in server sends to the game window, which is why even the "64 x Diamond" lines are missing there. ShulkerPeek sends the contents the way a vanilla server does. On vanilla servers the grid works too; on other Horizon servers without the mod, boxes look empty.
